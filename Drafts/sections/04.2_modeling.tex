\subsection{Modeling changes in interethnic marriage}

The descriptive results, while informative, do not account for structural
conditions of the marriage market--specifically, the changing ethnic and
educational compositions of husbands and wives.
We therefore employ log-linear models to examine assortative mating preferences
that cause observed marriage patterns to deviate from patterns expected under
random sorting.
